## Asset check (2026-11-09)

Every one of the 31 sets named in this list exists. Every character's rig and pose files load and render; each was checked in every move:
- **Front:** idle, wave, point, scared, the four standing-scared moods, jump, talk.
- **Side:** walk, run, sit, jump.

Every pose card was also rendered.

**Problems found and fixed during the check:**
- Side views:
  - The white shirts and sneakers had been eaten away by the background cut. The side views were redrawn on a green screen.
  - The sleeves were left behind on the side torsos. Fixed with new arm-less side drawings.
- Arms:
  - The deputy's sleeve texture showed his belt buckle.
  - Ms. Harlow's and Mom's sleeves had cuff stripes.
- Poses and heads:
  - Three poses were drawn too big: Dong crawling, Mom texting, and Ms. Harlow with her phone.
  - Cream crumbs and halos showed around the cut-outs in dark rooms.
  - The head cards included part of the shirt collar.

**Huddle:** in the floor-sitting huddle, every student and Ms. Harlow now has a live head on the drawn body. Each one plays the `huddle` clip with their own mood:
- shake
- sob (with tears)
- terror
- shock
- rock
- glance
- headshake
- whisper
- asleep

Standing characters use `standScared` (shake, hug, mouth, frozen, backaway). Every puppet has its own random phase, so no two move in step.

**Dark scenes:** use `setTint(0x8790ad)` or similar so the puppets sit in the room's light.

**Walks:** every character has their own walk and run, set in the `STYLE` table in `dong.js`:
- **Tyler:** loose and bouncy.
- **Priya:** brisk and neat.
- **Marcus:** big, slow and heavy.
- **The red-haired girl:** small quick steps with her head down.
- **The blond boy:** stiff, with his hands still.
- **Ms. Harlow:** upright.
- **Mom:** hurried.
- **The deputy:** measured, with his arms off his belt.

Each walker starts at a random point in the step, so a line of people never steps in unison. When a line has to keep its spacing, pass the same `pace` to everyone.

## Staging (2026-11-09)

Every set people appear in is a 3D room (`web/rooms/<set>.json`, built by `web/make_rooms.py`):
- **Camera:** matched to each painting from an object of known height, usually the 2.1 m door. A level camera's height is `h = Y·(v_floor − 0.5)/(v_floor − v_top)`.
- **Floor and walls:** the painting is projected onto a real floor and back wall; the hallways also get side walls.
- **Cut-outs:** furniture painted in the foreground is cut from the painting itself (AI matte → `web/masks/`) and stood up at its own depth. Characters go behind the desks, tables, railing and Dawes's desk.
- **Placement:** people are placed by where they stand on screen, spread out so everyone can be seen.
- **New art:**
  - `plate_class_students_empty`: a reverse angle of Room 214 for the class at their desks.
  - `dong_uni_sit_desk`.
  - `plate_bedroom_night_nobed`, plus Dong's `bed_asleep` and `bed_sit` cards. The bed is part of his card, so he can go from asleep to bolt upright.
- **Still flat pictures (no one in them):**
  - intercom, handle, window view, hallway floor and dim hallway, room 208
  - car, house, exteriors other than the lawn
  - the bedroom door close-ups
